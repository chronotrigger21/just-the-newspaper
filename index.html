BBC News

Dozens go on trial over North Macedonia nightclub fire that killed 63

BBC News

Thirty-five individuals and three institutions have gone on trial in North Macedonia for their alleged roles in a nightclub fire that claimed the lives of 63 people in March. The fire occurred at Club Pulse in Kocani during a concert when pyrotechnic devices ignited the ceiling, which was made of flammable material. Prosecutors argue that the tragedy resulted from a series of systemic failures, including unlawful licensing, lack of safety inspections, and blocked exits, which left many unable to escape. The trial is expected to be lengthy, with the presiding judge emphasizing the importance of transparency and accountability in addressing the collective responsibility for the disaster.

First-ever human case of rare bird flu strain confirmed in western US

Latest & Breaking News on Fox News

The Washington State Department of Health has reported the first human case of the H5N5 strain of avian influenza in the United States, involving an older adult from Grays Harbor County who is currently hospitalized. This case is notable as it marks the first human bird flu diagnosis in the country since February. The patient had a mixed backyard flock of domestic poultry that had exposure to wild birds, which is believed to be the source of the infection. Public health experts have assessed the risk to the general public as low, emphasizing that human transmission of avian influenza is extremely rare and has never been documented in the U.S. The Department of Health advises caution for those in contact with poultry or wildlife.
